### 1. [SubAero — Physics-Informed Digital Twin](https://github.com/Sridharshini-Crypto/SubAero.git)
> **Aerospace AI · 4-Stage Turbojet Diagnostics · HAL × IIT Indore Aerothon Top 25 Finalist**

A physics-informed digital twin for four-stage turbojet health monitoring, fusing Brayton cycle thermodynamic equations with neural networks for real-time degradation tracking, anomaly detection, and Remaining Useful Life (RUL) forecasting.

* **Core Stack:** `Python`, `PyTorch`, `Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)`, `Thermodynamic Modeling`, `Weibull Analysis`, `Three.js`
* **Pipeline:**
  ```
  Avionics Stream (CAN Bus) ──► Thermodynamic ODEs ──► Physics-Constrained NN ──► Weibull RUL Forecast ──► 3D Mission HUD
  ```
* **Key Focus:**
  - Thermodynamic loss constraints embedded into PyTorch to prevent unphysical predictions under severe aerodynamic edge cases.
  - Component-level degradation tracking across compressor, combustor, and high/low pressure turbines.
  - Ranked **Top 25 Finalist** out of 2,300+ national teams at HAL × IIT Indore Aerothon 2026.
